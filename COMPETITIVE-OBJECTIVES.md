# GoreeCloud Search — Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Search aims to combine strong ideas from federated metasearch, private-result browsing, independent indexing, customizable ranking, and source-backed answers while remaining a GoreeCloud-owned product.

Objectives:

- Make privacy an architectural default.
- Make third-party query disclosure explicit and controllable.
- Prefer GoreeCloud Index as the long-term first-party corpus.
- Keep federation optional and replaceable.
- Provide inspectable ranking and provenance.
- Support local, self-hosted, household, organizational, and larger deployments.
- Integrate deeply with GoreeCloud Browser without silent provider substitution.
- Keep AI synthesis optional and evidence-backed.
- Avoid behavioral advertising profiles, paid ranking, and hidden cross-query personalization.
